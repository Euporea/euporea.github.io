Nobody Online

Nobody's online. Half a million bots are.

Nobody Online is an idle / incremental clicker RPG with auto-battled dungeons & raids, wrapped around a full MMORPG. Click to chop, mine and fish, automate the grind, craft new gear, then join a party and delve into auto-battle dungeons and raids. Around you, 500,000 simulated players chat, trade, party up, ninja your loot and climb the leaderboards.

Inspired by

  • World of Warcraft
  • RuneScape
  • EverQuest

Built with

  • Godot 4

  • Pixel art
  • PC / Steam

Features

Incremental clicker

Every click chips away at a tree, rock or fishing spot. Craft gear from what you gather and level skills all the way to 99.

Idle

Buy better tools and afk grind away. Your character keeps working while you idle, click to gather faster, or leave it running while you play a REAL MMO.

Auto-battler

Pick from 14 classes, be a tank, healer or DPS, and watch your character fight in side-view battles with live damage meters and upgrade your gear with Need/Greed loot rolls.

A simulated MMO

Party Finder, fully functional auction house, guilds, 3v3 arena and a living chat where bots talk to each other and you!

Upcoming Features

In progress

  • Community Stats: opt-in, anonymous totals across all players, with community infographics to follow.
  • New character art: detailed, hand-tuned character models for every race. The Warlock, with its Succubus and Incubus pets, is done, and the other classes are on the way.
  • Full animation sets: walk, attack and idle for the new models, in battles, raids and around the world.

Coming soon

  • Quest Board: pick up quests in Kettlecrown, such as slay, gather or craft, for bonus XP, gold and items. Quests keep progressing while you're offline.
  • Dungeon Log: see each dungeon's monsters, bosses and loot, filling in as you discover them.
  • Achievements: solo boss clears, challenge runs, and style-restricted kills like "all mages".
  • Item icons: real icons for items and loot chests in place of text.
  • Tutorial: a friendlier first hour for new adventurers.
  • More music: tracks for every zone, with separate volume sliders for music, effects and interface.

On the horizon

  • Legendary raid items with unique traits, and Epic gear sets with set bonuses.
  • Level 80–90 content: new dungeons and whatever lies beyond the Stormcrown Spire.
  • A living auction house: prices driven by player-bot supply and demand, price history and trend charts, and selling directly to buy requests.
  • A world that feels alive: world events, bot rivalries that rise and fall, and cheaters you can catch in the act. One might one-shot your raid boss and get banned live by a GM.
  • Smarter parties: synced parties that scale bots to the dungeon's tier for bonus rewards, auto-refilling your group's missing roles, and bribing busy players to join you.
  • Guild reputation: some guilds are full of ninja looters, so watch for warning signs and rumours in chat.
  • Arena seasons: seasonal rewards and a team rating leaderboard.
  • Richer themes: textured interface themes such as stone, wood, starlight and dragon scale, each with its own fonts.
  • Mac and Linux builds.
